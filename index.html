✦  On the 2026 Academic Job Market

I am a PhD student in the Department of Management Science at the Antai College of Economics and Management, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Wenhui Zhao. Currently, I am a visiting PhD student at the London Business School (advised by S. Alex Yang), October 2025 – October 2026.

I obtained my M.S. in Management Science and Engineering from Northeastern University in 2022 (advised by Zhong-Zhong Jiang) and my B.S. in Electronic Commerce from Xiangtan University in 2019.

My PhD research studies how firms and governments govern talent mobility to protect proprietary knowledge and sustain competitive advantage. My work has been published in Production and Operations Management, Naval Research Logistics, 管理科学学报, and others. I also serve as a reviewer for POM, JAIS, APJOR, and several Chinese journals.
